TONG QUAN UNG DUNG MONEYMATE / FINANCEMANAGER
=============================================

Tai lieu nay cap nhat theo cau truc workspace va ma nguon dang co. Ten hien thi
cua ung dung Flutter la MoneyMate; script SQL dat ten database FINANCE_MANAGER.


1. MUC DICH VA TINH TRANG TONG QUAN
-----------------------------------
MoneyMate la ung dung Flutter minh hoa quan ly thu chi ca nhan: ghi nhan giao
dich, lap ngan sach, xem thong ke va mo phong giao dich tu tai khoan ngan hang/
vi dien tu mau.

Hien tai:
- Frontend Flutter co giao dien va chuc nang demo; du lieu giao dich/ngan sach
  chi nam trong RAM, khong duoc luu sau khi dong ung dung.
- Frontend chua goi API backend.
- Backend ASP.NET Core da co project .NET 8, nhung phan dang duoc cau hinh de
  chay la MVC scaffold co Home/Privacy. Cac tep API/auth/data duoc de lai tu
  huong phat trien, chua duoc dang ky/noi vao luong chay hien tai.
- Cac tai khoan ngan hang va vi dien tu tren giao dien chi la du lieu mo phong
  phuc vu hoc tap; khong ket noi tai khoan tai chinh that.

   moi tai khoan. Giao dien hien canh bao ro rang day la du lieu mo phong.

6) Dong tien lien ket - screens/linked_flow_screen.dart
   Truy cap tu man hinh tai khoan; tong hop giao dich mo phong theo thang,

5. BACKEND .NET VA CAC THANH PHAN API DU KIEN
---------------------------------------------
Project:
- app_backend/app_backend.sln la solution.
- app_backend/app_backend/app_backend.csproj su dung Microsoft.NET.Sdk.Web va
  target net8.0; project la ASP.NET Core MVC.
- Program.cs hien dang AddControllersWithViews(), static files, routing,
  authorization middleware va route MVC mac dinh den Home/Index.
- HomeController.cs va Views/Home cung cap trang Index, Privacy, Error cua
  project scaffold. Chua thay controller REST API cho nghiep vu tai chinh.

Mot so tep model/API van co trong source nhung chua duoc noi vao MVC app:
- Auth/FirebaseAuthenticationHandler.cs: ma xu ly Bearer token Firebase va
  tao claim UID; Program.cs hien khong dang ky authentication scheme nay.
- DTOs/CreateTransactionRequest.cs: payload du kien cho tao giao dich.
- services/HouseholdAuthorizationService.cs: logic kiem tra thanh vien/quyen
  ho gia dinh; TransactionService.cs co doan logic yeu cau duyet chi.
- Models/: Account, User, Household, HouseholdMember va Transaction; cac enum
  dinh nghia trong Enum.cs.
- Chua thay DbContext, migrations hoac controller de doc/ghi cac model.
- Cac service/Auth tham chieu cac namespace va lop DB/model chua co day du trong
  project hien tai; can hoan thien dependency va kiem tra build truoc khi xem
  day la backend API san sang su dung.
- appsettings*.json la file cau hinh; can kiem tra va cung cap gia tri rieng
  cho moi moi truong, khong dua secret/credential vao tai lieu cong khai.


6. MO HINH DU LIEU VA SQL
-------------------------
app_doc/SQLQuery1.sql tao database FINANCE_MANAGER va cac bang:
- Users: nguoi dung, Firebase UID, email, ten hien thi, trang thai.
- Households: nhom gia dinh, nguoi tao va nguong can duyet.
- HouseholdMembers: vai tro Owner/Admin/Member, trang thai va quyen thanh vien;
  SQL co unique index de mot nguoi chi nam trong mot household dang hoat dong.
- Accounts: tai khoan cua nguoi dung, co the thuoc household; ho tro cash,
  bank demo, e-wallet demo.
- Categories: danh muc thu/chi.
- Transactions: thu/chi/chuyen tien, nguoi tao, tai khoan, danh muc, visibility,
  approval status, xoa mem va rowversion.
- TransactionShares: chia se giao dich den nguoi dung duoc chon.
- ApprovalRequests: yeu cau phe duyet cho giao dich.
- Budgets: han muc theo household/nguoi tao/danh muc, chu ky, che do va uu tien.

Schema SQL co khoa ngoai, unique/check constraints va index cho giao dich.
Enum BudgetPeriod hien dang dong bo ve gia tri voi SQL: 0=Monthly, 1=Weekly,
2=Yearly. SQL va enum can duoc giu dong bo neu thay doi schema.


7. CAU HINH VA LENH PHAT TRIEN
------------------------------
Flutter:
- Dart SDK constraint: ^3.9.2 trong app_frontend/pubspec.yaml.
- Dependencies: Flutter SDK, cupertino_icons, fl_chart; dev dependencies:
  flutter_test va flutter_lints.
- Thuc hien lenh tu thu muc app_frontend:
    flutter pub get
    flutter run
    flutter analyze
    flutter test
- Man hinh thong ke hien tai tu ve bieu do bang widget Flutter; fl_chart dang
  khai bao trong dependencies nhung chua duoc import trong cac tep nghiep vu da
  kiem tra.

Backend:
- Mo app_backend/app_backend.sln bang Visual Studio hoac chay project
  app_backend/app_backend/app_backend.csproj voi .NET 8 SDK.
- Properties/launchSettings.json co cac profile HTTP/HTTPS phuc vu chay local.
- Cau hinh DB/Firebase dang co trong appsettings; tuy nhien Program.cs hien
  chua doc/dang ky DbContext hay Firebase service.


8. KHOANG TRONG VA VIEC CAN LAM TIEP
------------------------------------
- Ket noi frontend voi API: thong nhat DTO, xac thuc Firebase, REST endpoints,
  repository/network layer va xu ly loi.
- Hoan thien backend: dang ky xac thuc, DbContext/provider SQL Server,
  entities con thieu, migrations, controller API va dependency packages; xu ly
  sai khac namespace giua cac tep scaffold va tep API.
- Them luu tru ben vung cho frontend hoac chuyen state sang API; hien tai dong
  ung dung se mat giao dich, ngan sach va trang thai demo.
- Test hien tai trong app_frontend/test/widget_test.dart van la test scaffold
  "Counter increments", khong phan anh giao dien MoneyMate; nen thay bang widget
  test cho cac man hinh/chuc nang hien tai.
- Neu sua enum/schema, cap nhat dong thoi app_doc/SQLQuery1.sql va backend; cac
  gia tri BudgetPeriod hien da khop nhau.
- Kiem tra appsettings va loai bo thong tin ket noi/secret rieng truoc khi chia
  se ma nguon.

Tai lieu mo ta trang thai ma nguon hien tai, khong khang dinh frontend va
backend da tich hop hoac da duoc kiem thu end-to-end.
